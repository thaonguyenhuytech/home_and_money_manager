Sun4Life

Sun4Life is an offline budget planner, money management, and home inventory application designed to help individuals and families manage their finances and household items in one place. It helps users track income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, shopping lists, and household items while keeping their data available without requiring a constant internet connection.

Features

  • Budget Planning and Tracking
  • Zero-Based Budgeting
  • Income and Expense Tracking
  • Spending and Transaction Management
  • Savings and Financial Goals
  • Financial Reports and Insights
  • Home Inventory Management
  • Household Item Tracking
  • Shopping List Management
  • Warranty and Maintenance Tracking
  • Purchase and Item Details

Budget and Money Management

Sun4Life helps users plan and manage their money by creating budgets, tracking income and expenses, and organizing financial transactions. Users can also use zero-based budgeting to assign their available money to specific categories and purposes.

Savings and Financial Goals

Users can create savings goals and monitor their progress over time. This makes it easier to plan for future expenses and keep track of progress toward personal financial goals.

Financial Reports and Insights

Sun4Life provides clear summaries, charts, and reports to help users understand their spending habits, income, expenses, savings, and overall financial situation.

Home Inventory

Sun4Life helps users organize and keep track of household belongings. Users can create a digital inventory of their items, including photos, purchase details, locations, and other useful information.

Shopping and Household Tracking

Users can manage shopping lists and keep track of household supplies and other items. Household items can also include warranty information, maintenance schedules, and replacement reminders.

Offline and Private

Sun4Life is designed to work offline, allowing users to access and manage their financial and household data without requiring a constant internet connection.

Your personal financial and household information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to use a backup or restore feature.

Google Drive Backup

Sun4Life allows users to back up and restore their application data using Google Drive.

Backups are stored securely in the application's private Google Drive App Data Folder, which is hidden from the user's normal Google Drive files.

The application only accesses backup files that it creates and manages. It cannot view, modify, or access other files stored in your Google Drive.
